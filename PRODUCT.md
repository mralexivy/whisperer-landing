# PRODUCT.md — Whisperer (marketing site, whispererapp.com)

## What it is
Whisperer is a native macOS app (Apple Silicon, macOS 26+) for AI meeting notes and dictation that runs on the Mac.
Pillars, in selling order: meeting notes (call detection, both sides recorded, no bot, on-device speaker diarization,
calendar-named speakers, AI notes, Ask AI), bring your own AI (Claude / ChatGPT / Codex / Gemini plans, API keys, or a
free built-in offline Qwen3.5 4B model), a local MCP server so AI apps can read meetings, calendar sync (Google, Outlook),
and hold-Fn dictation with live preview in 97 languages.

## Who buys
- Individuals: knowledge workers, founders, consultants, developers on Mac who hate meeting-note subscriptions and bots.
- Organisations: teams and enterprises that want meeting notes for every employee without a per-seat monthly SaaS bill,
  without a new vendor processing meeting audio, and using the AI tools the company already pays for.

## Price
$2.99 app (dictation). Pro $14.99 one-time in-app purchase = everything else. No subscription, no account.
