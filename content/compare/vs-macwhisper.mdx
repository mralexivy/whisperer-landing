Pick **MacWhisper** if your work is transcribing files: interviews, lectures, podcasts and videos, with exports and subtitles. Pick **Whisperer** if you want your calls turned into notes without lifting a finger, plus dictation for everything else, using the AI you already pay for, for [$2.99 plus $14.99 once](/pricing).

## The short version

Both apps run Whisper on your Mac, and both are one-time purchases. They grew from different starting points.

MacWhisper started as a file transcriber. You drop in a recording, it gives you a transcript you can edit, search and export. It has grown a lot over time and is one of the most complete transcription apps on the Mac. It now also records meetings in Zoom, Teams, Webex and other apps with automatic start and end detection, captures audio from any app, and offers system-wide dictation.

Whisperer starts from the moment you talk. Hold Fn, speak, release, and paste the text with ⌘V. With Pro it notices when a call starts and takes notes for you: it records both sides with no bot in the call, separates speakers, and writes up decisions and action items. ## Where MacWhisper wins

- **Depth on files.** Batch queues, many input formats, editing the transcript, and exports such as subtitles for video. If you transcribe recordings for a living, MacWhisper is built around you.
- **A free version** to try before paying. Whisperer has no free tier.
- **Older Macs.** Whisperer needs Apple Silicon and macOS 26 Tahoe. If you're on an older system, MacWhisper may still run, so check its requirements.
- **Track record.** MacWhisper has been around for years.

## Where Whisperer wins

- **Calls take care of themselves.** When an app such as Zoom or Teams opens the microphone, Whisperer asks "Zoom call started. Take notes?" Meetings on your [calendar](/calendar) can record without asking.
- **Both sides of the call.** It records the call audio and your microphone, so speakers or headphones both work. A live transcript fills in while you talk.
- **Who said what.** Speakers are separated on your Mac, numbered as they first talk, and named from the invite in one click.
- **Notes, not only transcripts.** A title, overview, key topics, decisions, action items with owners, open questions and the next meeting. Ask AI answers questions with timestamps you can click.
- **Your own AI.** Use the built-in model offline, or sign in with your Claude, ChatGPT, Codex or Gemini plan, so there's no extra bill. If it's unavailable, the Mac takes over. Read more on [your AI](/ai).
- **MCP.** Turn on the [MCP server](/mcp) and Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Cursor or ChatGPT can search your meetings, read transcripts and add notes.
- **Everyday dictation** with live preview in 97 languages, a personal dictionary that learns from your corrections, and a searchable history.

## Who should choose which

**Choose MacWhisper if** you transcribe recorded files most of the time, you need subtitles or specific export formats, or you want a free version first.

**Choose Whisperer if** you spend your week in calls and want notes written for you, you want speaker names from your calendar, you'd like to use the AI plan you already have, or you want your AI tools to read your meetings through MCP. Some people use both: MacWhisper for a backlog of recordings, Whisperer for live calls and dictation.

## FAQ

### Is Whisperer a good MacWhisper alternative?

It depends on the job. For transcribing piles of audio and video files, with exports and subtitles, MacWhisper is the specialist. If you want live dictation and meeting notes that are written for you after each call, Whisperer is the better fit, for $2.99 plus a $14.99 one-time Pro purchase.

### How much does MacWhisper cost compared with Whisperer?

MacWhisper has a free version, and MacWhisper Pro is a one-time €64 license (public pricing, November 2026). Whisperer is $2.99 one-time for dictation and $14.99 one-time for Pro.

### Do both apps work offline?

Yes. Both transcribe on your Mac with Whisper models. Whisperer also offers Parakeet, Qwen3 ASR and WhisperKit, and its meeting notes can be written by the built-in Qwen3.5 4B model with no connection.

### Can Whisperer transcribe audio files?

Yes. File transcription for audio and video is part of the $2.99 app. MacWhisper goes deeper here, with batch work and more export options.
