Pick **JesType** if you want simple offline dictation on Mac and Windows with one payment, and you mainly speak one of Parakeet's 25 languages. Pick **Whisperer** if you're on a Mac, want offline dictation in 97 languages for $2.99, and also want [meeting notes without a bot](/meetings), your own AI and an [MCP server](/mcp) for a one-time $14.99.

## The short version

JesType keeps things small. It uses NVIDIA's Parakeet V3 models for 25 languages, plus Moonshine for streaming English, and pastes text into the field you're in. AI cleanup is optional and off by default.

Whisperer offers five offline engines, with Whisper Large V3 Turbo as the default. Hold Fn, speak, release, and the text is copied for you to paste with ⌘V. The $14.99 Pro purchase adds a meeting recorder that detects calls, records both sides and writes notes.

## Where JesType wins

- **Text goes into the field.** JesType pastes for you. With Whisperer's App Store build you press ⌘V.
- **Windows and older macOS.** JesType runs on Windows and on macOS 13.4 or later. Whisperer is Mac-only and needs macOS 26 Tahoe.
- **A trial.** You can try JesType for 48 hours before paying.
- **Fewer moving parts.** Two engines and a short settings list.

## Where Whisperer wins

- **More languages.** 97 with Whisper, detected as you speak, compared with Parakeet's 25.
- **No licence server.** Bought through the Mac App Store, so it never needs to phone home to keep working.
- **Files and history.** Transcribe audio and video files. Search past dictations, replay audio, re-transcribe with another engine.
- **Meetings.** Whisperer sees which app opened the microphone and asks "Zoom call started. Take notes?", or records automatically for meetings on your [calendar](/calendar). Speakers are named from the invite. Notes come from the built-in Qwen3.5 4B model or your Claude, ChatGPT, Codex or Gemini plan. See [your own AI](/ai).
- **MCP.** Claude, Cursor or Codex can search your meetings and transcripts from your Mac.

## Who should choose which

**Choose JesType if** you need Windows, you're not on macOS 26, or you want the simplest possible dictation tool.

**Choose Whisperer if** you speak a language outside Parakeet's list, you want file transcription and history, or you want your calls written up too. Start with [dictation](/features).

## FAQ

### How much does JesType cost?

JesType Personal is €14.95 one-time for one device, Pro is €34.95 for three, and Team is €49.95 for six (public pricing, November 2026). There is a 48-hour trial.

### Is Whisperer a good JesType alternative?

For dictation alone they are close: both run on the device and both are one-time purchases. Whisperer adds 97 languages with Whisper, file transcription, history, and with Pro, meeting notes, speaker names, your own AI and an MCP server.

### Does JesType need the internet?

Speech recognition runs on the device, but JesType checks its licence online, and paid licences work offline for up to 30 days between checks. Whisperer is bought through the Mac App Store and needs no licence check.

### Does Whisperer type into apps like JesType?

No. In the Mac App Store version, Whisperer copies your text and you paste with ⌘V. JesType pastes into the focused field.
