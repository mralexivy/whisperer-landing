Pick **Spokenly** if you want good local dictation for free, or cloud models and AI cleanup on a yearly plan, and you'd like text placed into the app for you. Pick **Whisperer** if you want offline dictation for $2.99 and also want [meeting notes without a bot](/meetings), your own AI and an [MCP server](/mcp) for a one-time $14.99.

## The short version

Spokenly is a generous dictation app. The free plan uses local Whisper and Parakeet models, needs no account, has no word cap, and lets you bring your own API keys for cloud transcription. Paid tiers add recording styles, settings sync, and Spokenly's own cloud models.

Whisperer is dictation plus a meeting recorder. Hold Fn, speak, release, and the text is copied for you to paste with ⌘V. The Pro purchase adds call detection for Zoom, Teams, Meet, Slack, Webex and FaceTime, calendar recording, speaker names, AI notes and Ask AI.

## Where Spokenly wins

- **Free.** Unlimited local dictation at $0, with no account.
- **Text goes into the field.** With Whisperer's App Store build you press ⌘V.
- **Cloud options.** Bring your own OpenAI, Deepgram or Groq key, or use Spokenly's cloud models on Pro.
- **iPhone.** Spokenly has a mobile app. Whisperer is Mac-only and needs Apple Silicon and macOS 26 Tahoe.

## Where Whisperer wins

- **Meetings.** Whisperer sees which app opened the microphone and asks "Zoom call started. Take notes?", or records on its own for meetings on your [calendar](/calendar). Both sides are captured. Nothing joins the call.
- **Who said what.** Speakers are identified on your Mac and named from the invite in one click.
- **Notes from your AI.** Overview, decisions, action items with owners and open questions, written by the built-in Qwen3.5 4B model or by your Claude, ChatGPT, Codex or Gemini plan. See [your own AI](/ai).
- **MCP.** Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Cursor and others can search your meetings and dictation history.
- **More engines.** Qwen3 ASR, WhisperKit and Apple Speech alongside Whisper and Parakeet, all on the device.

## Who should choose which

**Choose Spokenly if** you want free dictation, you want cloud models, or you dictate on iPhone too.

**Choose Whisperer if** you're on a Mac with macOS 26, you sit in calls, and you want dictation and meeting notes in one app with no subscription. Start with [dictation](/features).

## FAQ

### Is Spokenly free?

Yes. Spokenly's Free plan runs local Whisper and Parakeet models with no account and no word cap. Local+ is $29 once, and Pro is $12 a month billed $144 a year (public pricing, November 2026).

### Is Whisperer a good Spokenly alternative?

For dictation alone, Spokenly's free plan is hard to beat on price. Whisperer is worth it if you also want meeting notes without a bot, speaker names from your calendar, your own AI for notes, and an MCP server, all for a one-time $14.99.

### Do both work offline?

Yes, with local models. Spokenly's Pro plan adds cloud models, which need the internet. Whisperer's speech recognition is always on your Mac.

### Does Whisperer type into apps like Spokenly?

No. In the Mac App Store version, Whisperer copies your text and you paste with ⌘V.
