Pick **Wispr Flow** if you dictate on several devices, including Windows, iPhone and Android, and want AI-polished text typed straight into whatever app you're in, for a monthly fee. Pick **Whisperer** if you work on a Mac, want dictation that runs on your computer with no internet, and also want bot-free [meeting notes](/meetings), for [$2.99 plus $14.99 once](/pricing).

## The short version

Wispr Flow is a cloud dictation service. You speak, the audio goes to Wispr's servers, and cleaned-up text comes back into the app you're using. It is fast and polished, and it runs on Mac, Windows, iPhone and Android. Wispr also has a Notetaker for meetings that records on your Mac or Windows PC without a bot, with limited access on the free plan.

Whisperer runs on your Mac. Hold Fn, speak, release, and the words stream in as you talk. The text is copied, and you paste it with ⌘V. Transcription uses Whisper on the Metal GPU by default, with Parakeet, Qwen3 ASR, WhisperKit and Apple Speech as other options, all offline. The $14.99 Pro purchase adds meetings: call detection, calendar recording, speaker names, AI notes, Ask AI, your own AI, and an [MCP server](/mcp) for apps like Claude and Cursor.

## Where Wispr Flow wins

- **It types for you.** Text lands in the field you're in. With Whisperer's App Store build you press ⌘V. That is one extra key, and some people mind it.
- **Every device.** Mac, Windows, iPhone and Android with one account. Whisperer is Mac-only and needs Apple Silicon and macOS 26 Tahoe.
- **Cloud polish.** Wispr's server models rewrite and format text well.
- **Teams.** Shared dictionaries, a team workspace, admin controls and central billing. Whisperer has none of these.
- **A free tier.** You can use Wispr Flow for nothing within the weekly word limit. Whisperer starts at $2.99.

## Where Whisperer wins

- **Audio stays on your Mac.** Speech is transcribed on the device. No audio is uploaded. It works on a plane.
- **Meeting notes in the same app.** Whisperer notices a Zoom, Teams, Meet, Slack, Webex or FaceTime call by seeing which app opened the microphone. It records both sides, names speakers from your [calendar](/calendar), and writes decisions, action items with owners and open questions. Hold Fn mid-call to dictate; the meeting pauses and resumes.
- **Your AI, or none.** Notes come from the built-in Qwen3.5 4B model offline, or from Claude, ChatGPT, Codex or Gemini through the plan you have.
- **MCP.** Let Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Cursor or ChatGPT search your meetings and transcripts. Off by default, and only apps on your Mac can reach it.
- **File transcription and history.** Drop in an audio or video file. Search past dictations, replay audio and re-transcribe with another engine.
- **Pay once.** $17.98 total, compared with $144 a year.

## Who should choose which

**Choose Wispr Flow if** you dictate on Windows or your phone as much as your Mac, you want text typed directly into apps, or your team wants managed seats.

**Choose Whisperer if** you're on a Mac, you want speech handled on your computer, you also want meeting notes without a bot, or you'd rather pay once. Read more about [dictation in Whisperer](/features).

## FAQ

### Is Whisperer a good Wispr Flow alternative?

Yes, if you work on a Mac and want dictation that runs on your device with no subscription. Whisperer also records and summarizes meetings without a bot. Wispr Flow is the better choice if you need the same dictation on Windows, iPhone or Android, or want text typed straight into the app you're in.

### Does Wispr Flow work offline?

Wispr Flow processes speech in the cloud, so it needs an internet connection. Whisperer transcribes on your Mac and works with no connection.

### How much does Wispr Flow cost compared with Whisperer?

Wispr Flow Pro is $15 per user per month, or $12 a month billed yearly, and there is a free plan with a weekly word limit. Whisperer is $2.99 one-time for dictation and $14.99 one-time for Pro (public pricing, November 2026).

### Does Whisperer type into my apps automatically?

No. In the Mac App Store version, Whisperer copies your text and shows a short "Copied ⌘V" toast. You press ⌘V to paste it where you're typing.
