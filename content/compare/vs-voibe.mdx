Pick **Voibe** if you want dictation that types into your apps on both Mac and Windows, with on-device processing on Apple Silicon Macs. Pick **Whisperer** if you're on a Mac, want the same kind of offline dictation for $2.99, and also want [meeting notes without a bot](/meetings), your own AI and an [MCP server](/mcp) for a one-time $14.99.

## The short version

Voibe and Whisperer both run Whisper on an Apple Silicon Mac, so your voice stays local. On Windows, Voibe uses a cloud mode. Voibe sells dictation as a monthly, yearly or lifetime plan, and its Work tier adds meeting notes. Whisperer is a $2.99 app with a $14.99 one-time Pro purchase.

For dictation alone they are close peers. Both also take meeting notes without a bot: Voibe on its Work plan, Whisperer with Pro. Whisperer notices a Zoom, Teams, Meet, Slack, Webex or FaceTime call, records both sides, names speakers from your calendar, and writes notes.

## Where Voibe wins

- **Text goes into the field.** Voibe types for you. Whisperer's App Store build copies the text, and you press ⌘V.
- **Windows.** Voibe runs on Mac and Windows. Whisperer is Mac-only and needs Apple Silicon and macOS 26 Tahoe.
- **Older Macs and macOS.** If you can't move to macOS 26 yet, Whisperer won't run.
- **A free plan** for light use, and a 7-day trial of the Work plan.
- **Meeting notes on Windows.** Voibe Work's meeting notes also run on Windows.

## Where Whisperer wins

- **Calls found for you.** Whisperer sees which app opened the microphone and asks "Zoom call started. Take notes?", or records automatically for meetings on your [calendar](/calendar). Nothing joins the call.
- **Notes you can act on.** Overview, decisions, action items with owners, open questions, the next meeting, and Ask AI with timestamped sources.
- **Your AI.** The built-in Qwen3.5 4B model works offline, or use the Claude, ChatGPT, Codex or Gemini plan you already pay for. See [your own AI](/ai).
- **MCP.** Let Claude, Cursor or Codex search your meetings and transcripts from your Mac.
- **Four engines.** Swap between Whisper, Parakeet, Qwen3 ASR and WhisperKit, and re-transcribe old recordings with another one.
- **Price.** $17.98 once covers everything, where Voibe's meeting notes start at $149 a year.

## Who should choose which

**Choose Voibe if** you work on Windows as well as a Mac, you want meeting notes on Windows, or you want text typed directly into apps.

**Choose Whisperer if** you're on macOS 26, you'd like your calls written up, and you prefer one small payment. Start with [dictation](/features).

## FAQ

### How much does Voibe cost?

Voibe has a free plan. Dictation is $9.90 a month, $99 a year or $199 one-time. Work is $19 a month, $149 a year or $399 one-time (public pricing, November 2026).

### Is Whisperer a good Voibe alternative?

Yes, if you're on a Mac with macOS 26 and want on-device dictation for $2.99, plus meeting notes, calendar recording, speaker names, your own AI and an MCP server for a one-time $14.99. Voibe is the better pick if you also need Windows, or want text typed into your apps.

### Do both work offline?

On a Mac, yes. Voibe runs on the device on Apple Silicon Macs; on Windows and Intel Macs it uses a zero-retention cloud mode that needs a connection. Whisperer always runs on your Mac.

### Does Whisperer type into apps like Voibe?

No. In the Mac App Store version, Whisperer copies your text and you paste with ⌘V.
