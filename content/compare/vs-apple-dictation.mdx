Stick with **Apple Dictation** if you dictate short messages, it gets your words right, and you like that it types straight into the field for free. Switch to **Whisperer** if you dictate longer text, use names and jargon Apple keeps getting wrong, speak more than one language, or want [meeting notes without a bot](/meetings). Dictation is $2.99 once, and Pro is $14.99 once.

## The short version

Apple Dictation is already on your Mac. Press the dictation key, talk, and text appears where your cursor is. On Apple Silicon it runs on the device for many languages. For a quick reply in Messages, it's hard to beat free and zero setup.

Whisperer is a separate app built for people who dictate a lot. Hold Fn, speak, release. Words stream in while you talk, and the finished text is copied for you to paste with ⌘V. It uses Whisper on the Metal GPU by default, with Parakeet, Qwen3 ASR, WhisperKit and Apple's own Speech engine as alternatives, all offline. Pro adds a meeting recorder, your own AI and an [MCP server](/mcp).

## Where Apple Dictation wins

- **It's free and already there.** No purchase, no download, no settings.
- **It types for you.** Text goes straight into the field. With Whisperer you press ⌘V.
- **Every Apple device.** The same dictation works on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Whisperer is Mac-only and needs Apple Silicon and macOS 26 Tahoe.
- **Voice commands for editing.** Apple has spoken commands for punctuation and selection that Whisperer doesn't try to copy.

## Where Whisperer wins

- **Long, natural speech.** Whisper models were trained on a large amount of real-world audio and handle rambling, accents and long passages well.
- **Your words.** Add names, product terms and acronyms to the dictionary, and Whisperer learns new ones from the corrections you make. Prompt words help the engine expect them.
- **Languages.** 97 with Whisper, detected as you speak, so you can switch between English and Spanish without touching a menu. See the [multilingual guide](/blog/multilingual-dictation-guide).
- **Everything you said is kept.** Search past dictations, replay the audio, and re-transcribe with another engine. Statistics show words per minute, apps, languages and peak hours.
- **Files.** Drop in a voice memo, lecture or video and get a transcript.
- **Meetings (Pro).** Whisperer notices a Zoom, Teams, Meet, Slack, Webex or FaceTime call, records both sides, names speakers from your [calendar](/calendar), and writes decisions and action items. Notes come from a local model or from the [AI plan you already have](/ai).

## Who should choose which

**Stay with Apple Dictation if** you dictate a sentence or two at a time, you also dictate on iPhone, and the accuracy is fine for you.

**Choose Whisperer if** you dictate for minutes at a time, Apple keeps getting names wrong, you speak several languages, or you want your calls written up for you. Start with [dictation](/features) for $2.99 and add Pro when you need it. ## FAQ

### Is Apple Dictation good enough?

For short messages and notes, often yes. It is free, built in, and types straight into the field. People who dictate long text, use names and jargon, switch languages or want meeting notes tend to outgrow it.

### Is Whisperer more accurate than Apple Dictation?

Whisperer's default engine is Whisper Large V3 Turbo, which handles long, natural speech and names well, and you can teach it words with a personal dictionary. Results depend on your voice, microphone and language, so try both on your own speech.

### Does Whisperer cost money?

Yes. Dictation is $2.99 one-time on the Mac App Store. Pro is a $14.99 one-time in-app purchase that adds meeting notes, calendar recording, speaker names, your own AI and an MCP server. There is no subscription.

### Does Whisperer type into apps like Apple Dictation does?

No. Apple Dictation types into the field. Whisperer copies the text and shows a "Copied ⌘V" toast, and you paste with ⌘V.
