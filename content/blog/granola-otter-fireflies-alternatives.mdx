If you want an alternative to Granola, Otter or Fireflies on a Mac, the real question is who reads your notes. All three are cloud services built around sharing: they shine when a team keeps meetings in one place. If the notes are mostly for you, and you'd rather keep recordings on your own computer and skip a monthly fee, a Mac app such as [Whisperer](/meetings) does the same core job (record, transcribe, summarize) for $2.99 plus $14.99 once.

## The quick answer

- **Pick Granola** if you like typing your own rough notes during a call and want AI to fill them out afterwards, with templates and team sharing.
- **Pick Otter** if you want live transcripts that colleagues can follow, plus phone apps for recording in person.
- **Pick Fireflies** if your company runs on a CRM and wants call notes pushed into it automatically, across many meeting platforms.
- **Pick Whisperer** if you work on a Mac, want the audio to stay there, want to use the Claude or ChatGPT plan you already pay for, and don't want another subscription.

## Granola

Granola is a notes app that listens. You open a note before the call, type the few lines that matter to you, and after the call Granola rewrites them into full notes using the transcript. Nothing joins the meeting.

### Where Granola is strong

- **The editor.** Mixing your own shorthand with the AI draft is Granola's best idea. If you think by typing, it feels natural.
- **Templates** for common meetings, such as a one-on-one or a customer call.
- **Sharing.** Send a note by link, or keep notes in a shared team space.
- **More than the Mac.** Granola has apps for Windows, iPhone and Android.
- **MCP.** Business includes an MCP server, so Claude or ChatGPT can read your Granola notes.

### What to weigh

Audio is sent to cloud transcription providers and deleted after transcription, and transcripts and notes are stored in Granola's cloud. Granola keeps no recording, so there is no playback. Business is a per-seat monthly fee, and the free plan only shows the last 30 days of notes. The AI that writes your notes is Granola's choice, not yours.

## Otter

Otter is one of the oldest names in meeting transcription. Its assistant can join your Zoom, Teams or Google Meet call and transcribe it live, so other people in the meeting can follow along. A desktop app for Mac and Windows can also record without a bot.

### Where Otter is strong

- **Live, shared transcripts.** Colleagues can watch the transcript as it happens and highlight lines.
- **Phone, desktop and web apps.** Record an in-person conversation on your iPhone or Android phone, or use the Mac and Windows apps.
- **An MCP server** on every plan, so Claude or ChatGPT can read your Otter meetings.
- **A team workspace** where transcripts are searchable across people.

### What to weigh

A bot in the participant list changes some meetings. Clients notice it, and some companies block it. Every plan below Business has monthly transcription minute limits (Free is 300 minutes a month, with a 30-minute cap per conversation). Transcription supports six languages. Processing happens in the cloud.

## Fireflies

Fireflies sends its assistant, Fred, into calls on most major platforms and turns them into transcripts, summaries and searchable records for a team. On paid plans a Mac and Windows desktop app records without the bot.

### Where Fireflies is strong

- **Integrations.** Fireflies connects to CRMs, project tools and chat apps, so call notes land where a sales or support team already works.
- **Conversation analytics**, such as talk time and topic tracking, which managers use for coaching.
- **Wide platform support**, since a bot can join almost any web meeting, plus web, desktop and phone apps.
- **An MCP server** so AI tools such as Claude and ChatGPT can query your meetings.

### What to weigh

Like Otter, the bot is visible to everyone on the call unless you use the desktop app. It's a team product at heart, priced per seat each month, and your audio is processed in the cloud.

## Whisperer, the bot-free Mac option

Whisperer is a Mac app that notices when a call starts and records it from your computer. Here is what happens on a normal Tuesday:

1. Zoom opens the microphone. Whisperer sees which app is using it and asks "Zoom call started. Take notes?" For meetings on your [Google or Outlook calendar](/calendar), it can start recording on its own.
2. It records the call audio and your microphone, so both sides are captured whether you use speakers or headphones. A live transcript fills in as people talk.
3. A small bar at the edge of the screen, the Island, lets you pause, stop or open the meeting.
4. After the call, speakers are separated on your Mac and numbered in order of who spoke first. One click names them from the calendar invite.
5. You get a title, an overview, key topics, decisions, action items with owners, open questions and the next meeting.

Ask AI answers questions about a meeting with timestamps you can click. Search covers the full transcript of every meeting. You can read the whole flow in [AI meeting notes on Mac without a bot](/blog/ai-meeting-notes-mac).

### Your AI, your choice

The notes are written by the AI you pick on the [LLM page](/ai): the built-in Qwen3.5 4B model, which runs offline at no cost, or your existing Claude, ChatGPT, Codex or Gemini plan, or an API key. Usage goes against the plan you already pay for. If that AI isn't available, the Mac takes over. Only the text transcript ever goes to a cloud AI, and you can keep any meeting local.

### MCP for the apps you already use

Turn on the [MCP server](/mcp) and Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Cursor, Codex or ChatGPT can list your meetings, read transcripts and add notes labelled with their own name. It's off by default and only apps on your Mac can reach it.

### Where Whisperer falls short

Be clear about this before you switch:

- **Mac only.** It needs Apple Silicon and macOS 26 Tahoe. No Windows, iPhone or web app.
- **No team workspace.** Notes live on your Mac. To share, you copy or export.
- **No CRM integration** like Fireflies has.
- **No visible recorder.** Since nothing joins the call, other people won't see that you're taking notes. Whisperer shows a one-time consent reminder, and you're responsible for telling people and asking where the law requires it. More in [meeting notes without a bot](/blog/meeting-notes-without-bot).

## How to choose

Start with two questions. Do other people need to read your notes in a shared app? If yes, pick one of the three cloud services and try its free plan for a week of real meetings. Is the problem a bot in the call, a monthly bill, or audio leaving your computer? Then a Mac app is the better fit.
