Email is mostly typing the same kinds of sentences over and over. "Thanks for sending this." "Let me know if you have questions." "Can we schedule a call?" Speaking these feels faster than typing them, and it is.

## Why This Works

Typing: 40-60 words per minute. Speaking: 130-150. Email is plain language. No symbols, no formatting. Just talk.

<Callout type="info">
A 100-word email takes:
- **Typing:** 1.5-2.5 minutes
- **Dictation:** 40-50 seconds

If you send 40 emails a day, that adds up to **40-80 minutes**.
</Callout>

## Works in any email app

Hold Fn, speak, release. Whisperer copies the text and shows a **Copied ⌘V** toast. Press ⌘V to paste it into the reply. That works in any email client:

- **Gmail** (Chrome, Safari, Arc)
- **Apple Mail**
- **Outlook**
- **Superhuman**
- **Hey**

### Let AI lay out the email

In Workspace, open **AI Modes** and set the post-process mode to **Email** while you clear your inbox. You speak the content and it comes back with a greeting, paragraphs and a sign-off. Switch back to plain dictation when you're done. The rewriting runs on the AI you pick on the LLM page, which by default is a model on your Mac.

## Mobile Drafts

Whisperer is Mac-only, but most email clients sync drafts. Start an email by voice on your Mac, finish on your phone. Or the other way around.

## FAQ

### Won't it make mistakes?

Whisper handles conversational English well. It's accurate for everyday email, but scan before you hit send.

### What about sensitive emails?

Speech is turned into text on your Mac. With the built-in AI model nothing is sent anywhere, which makes it fine for confidential mail.

### Does formatting work?

Whisperer gives you plain text to paste. Your email client handles formatting, and plain text is usually what you want anyway.
