The cheapest good dictation apps for Mac in 2026 are Apple Dictation (free), Spokenly's free tier, Whisperer ($2.99 one time), JesType (€14.95 one time) and VoiceInk ($25 one time). All of them can turn speech into text on your Mac. You can spend $249.99 on a lifetime dictation license, but for dictation alone the expensive apps aren't automatically better.

Prices below are public pricing, November 2026, for one person.

For reference, the pricier options: Superwhisper Pro is $8.49 a month, $84.99 a year or $249.99 lifetime. Wispr Flow Pro is $15 a month, or $12 a month billed yearly. Voibe's Dictation plan is $99 a year or $199 one time.

## 1. Apple Dictation

**Price:** free | **Best for:** quick notes

Apple's built-in option. Press the dictation key and talk. On Apple Silicon it runs on the device for supported languages.

- **Good:** free, no setup, works in any text field.
- **Missing:** no history, no personal dictionary, no file transcription, no choice of model. It can trip over names and jargon.

**Bottom line:** fine for short notes. You'll notice the limits once you dictate a lot.

## 2. Whisperer

**Price:** $2.99 one time | **Best for:** anyone who dictates daily and wants more than the basics

Hold Fn, speak, release. A live preview shows words as you talk. When you let go the text is copied and a "Copied ⌘V" toast appears, so you press ⌘V to paste it.

What $2.99 includes:

- Offline engines: Whisper (default Whisperer V3, about 547 MB), Parakeet, Qwen3 ASR and WhisperKit, with Apple Speech driving the live preview
- 97 languages with Whisper, detected automatically
- Fast Polish for punctuation, filler words and paragraphs
- A personal dictionary that learns from your corrections
- File transcription for audio and video
- History with search and playback, plus statistics
- Hold-to-talk, toggle and hands-free modes

**Pro, $14.99 once,** is optional. It adds [meeting notes](/meetings) for Zoom, Teams, Meet and other calls without a bot, calendar recording, speaker names, AI summaries with [your own AI](/ai) and an [MCP server](/mcp). You don't need it for dictation.

**Bottom line:** the most features for the lowest one-time price here. It needs Apple Silicon and macOS 26 Tahoe, and you paste with ⌘V instead of the text appearing on its own.

## 3. Spokenly

**Price:** free; Local+ $29 once; Pro $144 a year | **Best for:** trying local dictation for nothing

The free tier runs local Whisper and Parakeet models with no word cap, and you can plug in your own API keys for cloud models. Local+ adds extras such as recording styles and settings sync. Pro adds Spokenly's own cloud models.

**Bottom line:** a generous free tier that also runs on Windows, Linux and iPhone. Its own high-accuracy cloud models are on the paid Pro tier.

## 4. JesType

**Price:** €14.95 once (one device) | **Best for:** Mac and Windows users who want simple local dictation

Runs Parakeet V3 (25 languages) and Moonshine (English streaming) locally, with a 48-hour trial. Paid licenses check in online from time to time.

**Bottom line:** cheap and simple, and it also runs on Windows. Fewer languages than Whisper's 97.

## 5. VoiceInk

**Price:** $25 once for one Mac ($39 for two, $49 for three) | **Best for:** people who like open source

A clean, open-source (GPL v3) dictation app built on local Whisper and Parakeet models, now with an iPhone app too.

**Bottom line:** solid, simple and local. It sticks to dictation.

<Callout type="info">
Why can a local app be this cheap? There are no servers to pay for. Speech recognition runs on your Mac's own chip, so there's no per-minute cost to pass on.
</Callout>

## FAQ

### What is the cheapest good dictation app for Mac?

Apple Dictation is free and built in. Spokenly has a free local tier. Whisperer costs $2.99 one time and includes a choice of offline speech engines, a personal dictionary, file transcription, history and statistics.

### Do I need a subscription for offline dictation on a Mac?

No. Whisperer ($2.99), JesType (€14.95) and VoiceInk ($25) are one-time purchases that run speech recognition on your Mac.
