The text you want depends on where it's going. A Slack reply wants a quick, casual line. An email wants full sentences. A prompt to an AI coding agent wants plain, specific instructions. Dictation apps handle this in different ways, so here is exactly what Whisperer does, and what you do yourself.

**In short:** Whisperer has one per-app behaviour, a setting called **Match the app you're writing in**. Everything else (AI modes, language, dictionary) is one choice that applies everywhere until you change it.

## What "Match the app you're writing in" does

Open **Settings**, find the **Text Output** card, and you'll see the switch. It's on by default. The description says what it does: *skips the full stop on one-line chat messages.*

When you start dictating, Whisperer notes which app is in front. If it's a chat app and your message is a single sentence, the closing full stop is left off, because nobody types one in a quick chat reply and it can read as curt.

|---|
| Slack |
| Messages |
| WhatsApp |
| Discord |
| Telegram |
| Microsoft Teams |
| Spark |

It's deliberately careful. The full stop stays when:

- the message has more than one sentence
- it ends in a question mark, exclamation mark or an ellipsis
- the last word is an abbreviation ("etc.", "vs."), an initial, a number, or something like `config.js`
- you're in any app that isn't on the chat list

## What it doesn't do

Whisperer also recognises editors and terminals (Xcode, VS Code, Cursor, Terminal, iTerm2, Warp, JetBrains, Sublime Text) and writing apps (Mail, Notes, Word, Outlook, Notion, Obsidian). Today, the text in those apps is the same as it would be anywhere else. There's no automatic switch to a different mode, language, dictionary or AI rewrite per app.

That's a choice in favour of predictability: what you see in the live preview is what you get, whichever window you paste into.

## Setting up the rest yourself

Since modes don't switch on their own, it helps to know which choice to make for which kind of work. Every setting below applies to all apps until you change it.

| Where you're writing | What to use |
|---|---|
| Slack, Teams, Messages | Plain dictation with Fast Polish. Match the app handles the full stop. |
| Gmail, Mail, Outlook | Plain dictation for quick replies; the **Email** AI mode for a batch of longer emails |
| Notion, Obsidian, docs | Plain dictation, or **Format** when you want headings and lists |
| Cursor, Claude Code, Codex | Plain dictation. Speak the prompt in full sentences. |
| Comments, docstrings, PR text | Plain dictation, or the **Coding** AI mode to tighten it into technical prose |

AI modes come with Pro and use the AI you pick on the LLM page: the built-in model that runs offline on your Mac, or your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini plan. See [AI writing](/features/ai-writing).

## How text gets into each app

The delivery is the same everywhere: hold **Fn** (or your own shortcut), speak, release. Whisperer copies the text and shows a **Copied ⌘V** toast. Press **⌘V** to paste. Because it's a paste, it works in any app with a text field, including web apps in Safari or Chrome.

There's one more option in the same Text Output card: **Add space after text**, which adds a trailing space so you can keep typing straight after the pasted words.

## When to turn Match the app off

Turn it off if you want every message to end with a full stop, for example if your team's chat style is formal or you paste chat text into documents later. The switch is the only control; there's no per-app list to edit.

## FAQ

### Does Whisperer have per-app profiles?

Not as a settings system. It has one setting, Match the app you're writing in. When it's on, a one-line message dictated into a chat app such as Slack, Messages, WhatsApp, Discord, Telegram, Teams or Spark is pasted without a final full stop. Other apps get the same text as always.

### Can I use a different AI mode for each app?

Not automatically. The AI mode you pick stays on until you change it. Most people leave plain dictation on and switch to Email or Format for a batch of work.

### Why doesn't Whisperer remove the period in Mail or Notes?

Because in email, notes and documents a sentence should end with a full stop. The change only applies to one-sentence messages in chat apps, where people usually don't type one.
