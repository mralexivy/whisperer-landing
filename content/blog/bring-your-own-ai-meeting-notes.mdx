You can write AI meeting notes with the ChatGPT, Claude, Codex or Gemini plan you already pay for, instead of buying another subscription. Whisperer records and transcribes the call on your Mac, then hands only the text transcript to the AI you pick. If you'd rather not use a cloud AI at all, a built-in model on your Mac writes the notes offline for free. The app is $2.99 and the Pro upgrade with meetings is $14.99, both one-time.

## Why a second AI subscription feels wrong

Most meeting note apps bundle their own AI and charge for it every month. That made sense when few people had an AI plan. Now many people already pay for ChatGPT Plus, Claude Pro or Google AI Pro, and a meeting recorder that charges again for the same kind of model is paying twice for one job.

Whisperer splits the job in two. The Mac does the recording, the transcription and the speaker labels. The writing of notes goes to whichever AI you choose.

## How the LLM page works

Open the Workspace window and pick **LLM** in the sidebar. You get one list, grouped into three parts.

### On this Mac

The default is a built-in **Qwen3.5 4B** model. It's about a 3.2 GB download, runs on Apple Silicon and works offline. It's free, and it's also the fallback for everything else on the list. More on what it's good at in [offline meeting summaries with a local model](/blog/local-llm-meeting-summaries).

### Your AI subscriptions

- **Claude** uses your Claude Pro or Max plan through Claude Code.
- **ChatGPT** signs in with your Plus or Pro plan.
- **Codex** uses your ChatGPT plan through Codex.
- **Gemini / Antigravity** uses a Google AI Pro or Ultra plan.

Usage counts against the plan you already pay for. Whisperer doesn't add a charge on top.

### API keys

If you'd rather pay per use, paste an **OpenAI**, **Anthropic** or **Gemini** API key. Keys are stored in the macOS Keychain, not in a settings file.

## What leaves your Mac and what doesn't

The audio never leaves your Mac. Recording, transcription and speaker identification all happen locally.

When you pick a cloud AI, only the text transcript goes to that provider, under that provider's terms. When you pick **On this Mac**, nothing leaves at all. You can also keep a single meeting local, which is handy for a one-on-one or a call about a contract, while your other meetings use Claude or ChatGPT.

## When your AI isn't there

Plans hit limits. Sign-ins expire. Wi-Fi drops on a train. If the AI you chose can't be reached, the Mac takes over and the built-in model writes the notes. You still get notes for the meeting; they may just be a little plainer than what a large cloud model would write.

## Limits worth knowing

- **Plan limits apply.** A long all-hands transcript sent to Claude uses some of your Claude allowance. Heavy users on lower tiers may notice.
- **Quality varies by model.** Large cloud models catch more nuance in a two-hour planning meeting than a 4B model on a laptop.
- **Mac only.** Whisperer needs Apple Silicon and macOS 26 Tahoe. There's no Windows or web version, and no shared team workspace.
- **No meeting bot.** Nothing joins the call. That's good for privacy, but it means Whisperer has to run on the Mac you take the call on.

## FAQ

### Does using my ChatGPT or Claude plan cost extra?

No. Whisperer does not add a bill. The notes count against the usage limits of the plan you already pay for, the same way a chat would.

### What happens if my AI subscription is unavailable?

Whisperer falls back to the built-in Qwen3.5 4B model on your Mac, so the meeting still gets notes.

### Does my meeting audio go to the cloud AI?

No. Audio is transcribed on your Mac and stays there. Only the text transcript is sent to the cloud AI you chose, and you can keep any meeting fully local.

### Do I need an API key?

No. You can sign in with a Claude, ChatGPT or Google AI plan, or use the free model on your Mac. API keys are an option for people who prefer them.
