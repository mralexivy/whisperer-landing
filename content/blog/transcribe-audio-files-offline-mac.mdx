To transcribe an audio or video file offline on a Mac, open **File Transcription** in Whisperer's Workspace, drop the file in, and Whisperer turns it into text with a Whisper model running on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded and there's no per-minute charge. It's part of the $2.99 app and handles WAV, MP3, M4A, AAC, AIFF, FLAC, Opus, OGG, CAF, MP4, MOV and M4V.

Interview recordings, podcast episodes, voice memos and lectures all work the same way.

## Supported formats

| Kind | Formats |
|---|---|
| Audio | WAV, MP3, M4A, AAC, AIFF, FLAC, Opus, OGG, CAF |
| Video | MP4, MOV, M4V (the audio track is extracted for you) |

## How it works

<StepList>
<Step num="1" title="Open File Transcription">
Open Whisperer's Workspace window and choose **File Transcription** in the sidebar.
</Step>

<Step num="2" title="Drop your file">
Drag a file onto the drop zone or click **Choose File**. Files of any length work.
</Step>

<Step num="3" title="Transcription runs on your Mac">
Whisperer works through the file with your selected model. Everything stays on your Mac.
</Step>

<Step num="4" title="Get your text">
The transcript appears when it's done. Copy it, or keep it in your history to search later.
</Step>
</StepList>

<Callout type="tip">
**Speed depends on the model.** Whisperer V3, the default, is quick on Apple Silicon. Large V3 is slower but handles difficult audio better. See [which Whisper model to use](/blog/best-whisper-model-for-dictation).
</Callout>

## What people use it for

**Interviews and podcasts.** Turn recordings into searchable text you can quote from.

**Voice memos.** iPhone Voice Memos sync to your Mac through iCloud. Drop them into Whisperer.

**Lectures.** Transcribe recorded lectures for study notes. Add subject terms to your [personal dictionary](/blog/personal-dictionary-setup) first.

**Old meeting recordings.** If you have a Zoom recording saved as an MP4, it works too.

<Callout type="info">
**Recording a call live?** That's what Whisperer Pro's [meeting notes](/meetings) are for. It notices the call, records both sides without a bot, labels speakers and writes notes with decisions and action items. File transcription is for recordings you already have.
</Callout>

## Why offline

- **Privacy.** Audio stays on your Mac, which matters for confidential interviews, legal recordings and medical content.
- **No per-minute pricing.** Cloud services often charge by the minute or cap minutes per plan. Here you paid $2.99 once.
- **Works anywhere.** Planes, trains and locked-down office networks.
- **No upload wait.** Long video files don't have to go anywhere before work starts.

## Accuracy

Results depend on:

- **The model.** Large V3 is the most accurate. Whisperer V3 is the best balance.
- **Audio quality.** Clear recordings with one speaker close to the mic work best.
- **Language.** Whisper covers 97 languages, with accuracy that varies by language.
- **Your dictionary.** Names and specialist terms come out right once they're in it.

## Compared with cloud transcription services

| | Whisperer | Typical cloud service |
|---|---|---|
| Price | $2.99 one-time | Monthly plan or per-minute pricing |
| Where audio goes | Stays on your Mac | Uploaded to the provider |
| Internet needed | No | Yes |
| File length | No limit | Often capped by plan |
| Sharing with a team | Copy and send the text | Usually built in |

Cloud services win on team sharing and on transcribing from a phone. If you work alone on a Mac, local transcription is simpler and private.

## FAQ

### Is there a length limit for file transcription?

No. Whisperer works through long files in chunks on your Mac. Longer files simply take longer.

### Do I need Pro to transcribe files?

No. File transcription is part of the $2.99 app. Pro ($14.99 once) adds live meeting recording with speakers and AI notes.
