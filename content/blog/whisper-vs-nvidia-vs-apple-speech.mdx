Whisperer gives you several offline speech engines. **Whisper** (the default, on the Metal GPU) covers 97 languages and is the safe choice for everyone. **NVIDIA Parakeet** runs on the Neural Engine and covers 25 European languages. **Qwen3 ASR** covers 31 languages. **WhisperKit** runs Whisper models through Core ML. **Apple Speech**, Apple's own recognizer in macOS 26, drives the live preview you see while you talk. Which engine is most accurate depends on the language, so the default is the one that works for every language.

All of them run on your Mac. After the model downloads, no internet is needed.

## Whisper, the default

OpenAI's open-source model, running locally through whisper.cpp on your Mac's GPU. No Python, nothing extra to install.

**Default model:** Whisperer V3, which is Large V3 Turbo quantized to about 547 MB. Quantizing (Q5) cuts the file to roughly a third of the full-precision 1.5 GB with little accuracy loss.

**Other sizes:** from Tiny (75 MB) to Large V3 (2.9 GB). See [which Whisper model to use](/blog/best-whisper-model-for-dictation).

**Use it when:**

- You want one engine that works for every language you speak
- You dictate in a less common language
- You're transcribing files and want the most accurate model (Large V3)

## Parakeet

NVIDIA's Parakeet model, running on Apple Silicon's Neural Engine. The Neural Engine is a separate part of the chip from the GPU, so it leaves the GPU free for other work like video editing.

**Use it when:**

- You dictate in one of its 25 European languages
- You'd like the GPU kept free
- You want to compare: for some languages it may suit your voice better than Whisper

**Limits:** 25 languages, against Whisper's 97.

## Qwen3 ASR

A speech model from the Qwen team, covering 31 languages. It's a larger download than Whisperer V3. It does well in English. For some languages, Hebrew for example, Whisper is clearly better.

**Use it when** you mostly dictate in English or another of its main languages and want to try an alternative to Whisper.

## WhisperKit

Whisper models compiled for Core ML. Same model family as Whisper, different runtime. Worth a try if you want Whisper's language coverage on Apple's Core ML stack.

## Apple Speech

Apple's on-device recognizer in macOS 26 Tahoe. In Whisperer it drives the **live preview**: the words that stream in while you're still speaking. Your chosen engine then produces the final text when you release the key. Apple's model needs no download from Whisperer and runs beside your engine instead of competing with it.

## How they fit together

Whatever engine you pick, the rest works the same:

- Your [personal dictionary](/blog/personal-dictionary-setup) and prompt words
- Filler word removal and Fast Polish
- AI modes such as Rewrite, Grammar and Translate
- History, statistics and file transcription

Switch engines in Settings without restarting. With Pro, [meeting transcription](/meetings) follows the engine you choose too.

## Which one to pick

| If you | Use |
|---|---|
| Want a default that works for every language | Whisper, Whisperer V3 |
| Need maximum accuracy on a hard recording | Whisper, Large V3 |
| Dictate in a supported European language and want the GPU free | Parakeet |
| Mostly dictate in English and like to compare | Qwen3 ASR |
| Prefer Core ML | WhisperKit |

The honest advice: start with the default, and only switch if you notice a specific problem in your language.

## FAQ

### Which speech engine should I use in Whisperer?

Start with Whisper and the default Whisperer V3 model. It covers 97 languages and is accurate everywhere. Try Parakeet if you dictate in one of its 25 European languages and want it on the Neural Engine, or Qwen3 ASR for its 31 languages.

### Is NVIDIA Parakeet better than Whisper?

Neither wins everywhere. Results depend on the language. Whisper covers far more languages (97 against Parakeet's 25), so it's the safer default. Parakeet runs on the Neural Engine, which leaves the GPU free.

### Do all the engines work offline?

Yes. Every engine in Whisperer runs on your Mac. After the model download, no internet is needed.
