Whisperer keeps a history of everything you dictate, on your Mac: the text, the audio, and stats like words per minute and the detected language. Open **Transcriptions** in Workspace to search it, pin or flag entries, play back what you said, copy the text again, or re-transcribe a recording with a different model. Nothing is uploaded.

## The Transcriptions view

<TranscriptionsMockup />

- **Timeline.** Every dictation in order, with the time, duration and first lines of text.
- **Detail panel.** Click a recording to see the full text, play the audio and check its stats.
- **Search.** Full-text search across everything you've dictated.
- **Pin and flag.** Pin the things you want to find again. Flag recordings to review later.

## Why keep a history

Many dictation apps throw the recording away once the text appears. That's fine until:

- **Something looks wrong.** Play the audio and hear what you actually said.
- **You need to find something.** "What was that address I dictated on Tuesday?" One search.
- **You forgot to paste.** You dictated, got distracted, and copied something else. The text is still in history.
- **The model got it wrong.** Click **Re-transcribe** to run the same audio through another model, such as Large V3.

<Callout type="tip">
History and audio stay under `~/Library/Application Support/Whisperer/` on your Mac. Whisperer has no analytics and doesn't upload recordings anywhere.
</Callout>

## Statistics

Workspace also has a **Statistics** page built from the same history:

<StatisticsMockup />

It shows total words, recording time, number of sessions, average words per minute, the apps you dictated into, daily activity, languages and peak hours. More in [tracking your dictation](/blog/dictation-productivity-statistics).

## Searching

The search bar at the top of Transcriptions matches the full text of every dictation. It runs locally, so results appear straight away with no network request.

## Copying text again

Click a transcription and copy it. Handy when you want to reuse a paragraph you dictated last week, or paste the same answer into a second email.

## Meetings have their own history

With Pro, recorded calls live on the **Meeting Notes** page, separate from dictation. Each meeting keeps its transcript, speakers, notes and audio, and you can search across all of them. You can set how long meeting audio is kept: never, 7, 30, 90 or 365 days. See [meetings](/meetings).
